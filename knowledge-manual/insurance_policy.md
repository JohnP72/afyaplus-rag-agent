# AfyaPlus Demonstration Insurance Policy

Document ID: AFYA-DEMO-INS-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Fictional training material

This document contains invented insurance rules for a student
software demonstration. It is not a real insurance policy.

## Outpatient consultations

The demonstration Basic Plan covers general outpatient consultations
at approved demonstration facilities.

The patient pays 20% of the eligible consultation charge.
The insurer pays 80%, subject to the remaining outpatient benefit.

The annual outpatient benefit limit is KES 50,000.

## Specialist consultations

Specialist consultations require a referral from a general clinician
and prior authorisation from the insurer.

Without the required referral and authorisation, coverage cannot
be confirmed.

## Diagnostic services

Laboratory tests requested by an attending clinician are covered
when they are included in the member's benefits.

MRI and CT scans require prior authorisation.

## Exclusions

The demonstration Basic Plan excludes cosmetic procedures performed
solely for appearance and services received after membership expires.

## Verification requirements

Before confirming eligibility, authorised staff must verify:
- Whether membership is active.
- Whether the requested service is included.
- Whether the facility is approved.
- Whether sufficient benefit remains.
- Whether referral or prior authorisation is required.

This manual alone does not establish an individual member's eligibility.
If member records are unavailable, explain the policy rules and refer
the eligibility decision to authorised insurance staff.

## Missing information

If this manual does not address a question, respond:
"Information not found in the available documents."

Do not invent coverage rules, benefit balances or authorisations.
